TIP 01
Start with Group A MCQs. Takes only 20 minutes and guarantees 11 marks.
TIP 02
In Group B, always attempt all 8 SAQs. Partial answers still get partial marks.
TIP 03
For LAQ: intro (1 line) → labelled diagram → description → economic importance → conclusion.
TIP 04
Even with weak theory, a neat labelled diagram alone gets you 4/8 marks in LAQ.
TIP 05
Use OR questions to pick whichever chapter you're more confident in. Never leave blank.

Ex-situ conservationConservation of organisms outside their natural habitat (botanical gardens, zoos, seed banks, DNA banks)
Ecological successionThe gradual and sequential change in the community structure of an ecosystem over time in a given area
Natural selectionThe process by which organisms with favorable variations survive and reproduce while those with unfavorable ones perish
SchizogonyAsexual reproduction by multiple fission, occurring in the liver and RBCs of the human host in Plasmodium
SporogonySexual reproduction phase of Plasmodium that occurs in the body of female Anopheles mosquito
Conjugation (Paramecium)Temporary union of two compatible individuals for exchange of micronuclei — a form of sexual reproduction
Potamodromous migrationMigration of fish from one freshwater body to another freshwater body for spawning (e.g., salmon, hilsa)
OothecaA protective egg case or egg mass produced by certain invertebrates including earthworm and cockroach
Industrial melanismThe phenomenon where dark-colored individuals of a species become more common in industrial areas due to natural selection
Common mistakes — do NOT make these
Botany mistakes
  • Writing floral formula without floral diagram — loses 2 marks automatically
  • Confusing in-situ (national parks) with ex-situ (botanical gardens)
  • Forgetting economic importance — always asked alongside structure
  • Drawing Mucor life cycle without labelling sporangiophore and zygospore

Zoology mistakes
  • Drawing Plasmodium life cycle with only human stage — must show mosquito stage too
  • Confusing trichocyst (Paramecium) with nematocyst (Cnidaria only)

  • Confusing nearest ancestor: answer is Homo neanderthalensis, not Homo erectus
  • Not labelling diagrams — minimum 4–5 labels needed for full marks
